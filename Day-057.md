## Topics Covered
- Starting/Stoping a service
- Checking status of a service
- Listing all the services

## Commands Practiced
| Command | What I Now Understand About It |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| `systemctl start <service name>` | For starting a service |
| `systemctl stop <service name>` | For stoping a service | 
| `systemctl status <service name>` | For checking status of a service  |
| `systemctl lists-units --type=service --all` | For listing all the services |
